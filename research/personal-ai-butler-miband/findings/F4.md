# F4: 个人开发者盈利模式与成本

## Findings

### [1] Apple App Store Small Business Program 将付费 App 与 Apple IAP 佣金降至 15%，适用于上一日历年 proceeds（扣除佣金及部分税费后）≤100 万美元的新旧开发者，个人开发者可直接申请。
- quote: "It features a reduced commission rate of 15% on paid apps and Apple In-App Purchases... Existing developers who made up to 1 million USD in proceeds in the prior calendar year for all their apps, as well as developers new to the App Store, can qualify for the program and the reduced commission."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [2] 在 App Store 内解锁功能/内容（含订阅、高级内容、解锁完整版）必须走 In-App Purchase，不得用自建 license key、二维码、加密货币等旁路收费；这是个人开发者做订阅/买断的硬约束。
- quote: "If you want to unlock features or functionality within your app, (by way of example: subscriptions, in-game currencies, game levels, access to premium content, or unlocking a full version), you must use in-app purchase. Apps may not use their own mechanisms to unlock content or functionality, such as license keys, augmented reality markers, QR codes, cryptocurrencies and cryptocurrency wallets, etc."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (App Review Guidelines, page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [3] App Store 允许自动续期订阅覆盖任意品类（不限工具类），但必须提供持续价值、订阅期 ≥7 天且跨用户设备可用；“打赏”必须通过 IAP 货币体系，不能用微信/支付宝收款码直接收小费。
- quote: "Apps may offer auto-renewable in-app purchase subscriptions, regardless of category on the App Store... you must provide ongoing value to the customer, and the subscription period must last at least seven days and be available across all of the user's devices... Apps may use in-app purchase currencies to enable customers to \"tip\" the developer or digital content providers in the app."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [4] 美国区以外（含中国区）App 内不得放外链/按钮把用户导向非 IAP 的数字商品购买通道；仅美国店面与特定区域 External Purchase Link entitlement 是例外。个人开发者若主攻中文市场，无法靠“官网支付宝/微信收款绕开 30%/15% 抽成”合规收款。
- quote: "In all other storefronts, except for the United States storefront, where this prohibition does not apply, apps and their metadata may not include buttons, external links, or other calls to action that direct customers to purchasing mechanisms other than in-app purchase."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [5] 健康/医疗类 App 在 App Store 受更严审查：健康测量精度/方法论须可披露验证，且须提示用户就医；这会限制“AI 管家直接给医疗建议”的定价包装（诊断/治疗表述易被拒）。
- quote: "Medical apps that could provide inaccurate data or information, or that could be used for diagnosing or treating patients may be reviewed with greater scrutiny... Apps must clearly disclose data and methodology to support accuracy claims relating to health measurements... Apps should remind users to check with a doctor in addition to using the app and before making medical decisions."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [6] 禁止用 HealthKit 等健康/医疗数据做定向或行为广告，因此“健康数据变现（广告）”在 iOS 渠道不可行，盈利应落在订阅/买断/增值而非数据广告。
- quote: "Ads displayed in an app... may not engage in targeted or behavioral advertising based on sensitive user data such as health/medical data (e.g. from the HealthKit APIs), school and classroom data (e.g. from ClassKit), or from kids (e.g. from apps in the App Store's Kids Category), etc."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [9] 微信支付商户开户支持「小微商户」主体：无营业执照的个人可用负责人身份证 + 辅助证明材料（门店场所/流动经营/线上商品证明）进件；个体工商户需营业执照+经营者身份证。因此个人主体可通过小微商户通道收订阅/单次付款，但仍是商户号而非“个人收款码”，可做合规持续收款的备选通道（Android/官网/H5）。
- quote: "3） 小微商户：负责人身份证及辅助证明材料（门店场所或流动经营或线上商品等相关证明，详见小程序指引）... 1） 个体工商户：营业执照、经营者身份证"
- url: https://pay.weixin.qq.com/static/help_guide/business_registration.shtml
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page live as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [10] 港大 DCE 研究（N=561 香港成人）：仅 10.0% 受访者曾购买过健康 App（56/561），90% 从未付费；价格档设为 HK$10/50/100（约 US$1.3/6.4/12.8）。“非常有用”的边际支付意愿 MWTP=HK$76.74，“完整安全隐私体系”MWTP=HK$59.77，医生态度“非常支持”MWTP=HK$183.38；曾购买过健康 App 的人群 MWTP 高出 HK$103.05。结论：健康 App 付费渗透极低，但隐私与专业背书能显著抬高支付意愿。
- quote: "Whether had bought health apps Yes 56 (10.0) No 505 (90.0) ... Very useful 0.64 ... 76.74 ... Complete security system 0.50 ... 59.77 ... Very supportive 1.53 ... 183.38 ... Whether had bought health apps ... Yes ... 0.86 ... 103.05. The mean MWTP across all significant variables was HK$70.46"
- url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10364011/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-07-03 (mHealth 2023;9:21; PMID 37492118)
- confidence: high

### [11] 以色列 mHealth 混合方法研究（N=121 问卷）：相信 App 能改善健康的人群中 55.2%（58/105）愿为一次性 50 NIS（约 US$15）付费，但 84.8%（89/105）拒绝 500 NIS（约 US$150）；对“最优医疗 App”（监测+方案+通知医生）约 44–46% 愿付最高档 100 NIS（约 US$30）。支付意愿与“相信有效”强相关（P=.02），且与 App 所提供收益正相关（rs=0.185, P=.04）。可作为中文/亚洲市场以外的一次性买断价格上限参考：约 $15 可测、$30 封顶、$150 基本无人买。
- quote: "Overall, 55.2% (58/105) of the group that believed that mobile apps can improve health stated that they would pay 50 NIS (US $15) for an app but not 500 NIS (89/105, 84.8%)... 44% (7/16) versus 46% (48/105) of participants from group 1 and 2, respectively, stated that they would be willing to pay the maximum amount asked: 100 NIS... Participants who believed that mHealth apps can enhance health (mean 1.96, SD 1.01) had a higher willingness to pay for the service (depending on price) than those who did not believe in app efficacy (mean 1.31, SD 0.87; t119=−2417; P=.02)."
- url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8527382/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-10-05 (JMIR Form Res 2021;5(10):e26125)
- confidence: high

### [12] 综合已核验来源，个人开发者可行的盈利结构在 iOS 上被强制为「订阅 / 买断（Non-Consumable IAP）/ 内购消耗品 / IAP 打赏」四类，抽成 15%（SBP）起步；Android 侧可用微信小微商户号做订阅扣款（避开应用内购），但中国安卓商店（应用宝/华为）抽成与个人开发者入驻条件本次未能从官方页核到。增值方向上，隐私合规 + 医生/专业背书 + 「非常有用」的功能感是抬价的三大杠杆（见 [10]），纯免费健康工具的付费转化约仅 10%（见 [10]）。
- quote: "The App Store Small Business Program is designed to accelerate innovation... a reduced commission rate of 15% on paid apps and Apple In-App Purchurchases" + "If you want to unlock features or functionality within your app... you must use in-app purchase."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (synthesis of fetched primary sources; page live 2026-09)
- confidence: medium
