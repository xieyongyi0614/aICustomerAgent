# F2: 可穿戴健康数据开放生态与合规

## Findings

### [1] HealthKit 是 iPhone/Apple Watch 健康数据的中心仓库，App 须经用户授权才能读写，且按数据类型细粒度授权
- quote: "HealthKit provides a central repository for health and fitness data on iPhone and Apple Watch. With the user's permission, apps communicate with the HealthKit store to access and share this data." / "The user must explicitly grant each app permission to read and write data to the HealthKit store. Users can grant or deny permission separately for each type of data."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit.md
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (Apple doc, Copyright 2026)
- confidence: high

### [2] HealthKit 强制读写用途描述键，缺失会导致请求授权时崩溃；设备锁屏时仓库加密，后台可能读不到但可写
- quote: "Apps must include usage descriptions, or it will crash when you request authorization to access HealthKit data. Include the NSHealthShareUsageDescription key to read, and the NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription key to write data to Healthkit." / "the device encrypts the HealthKit store when the user locks the device. As a result, your app may not be able to read data from the store when it runs in the background. However, your app can still write to the store, even when the phone is locked."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/protecting-user-privacy.md
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (Apple doc, Copyright 2026)
- confidence: high

### [3] HealthKit 数据禁止用于广告/出售给数据经纪商，向第三方披露须用户明示同意且对方须提供健康健身服务；必须提供隐私政策
- quote: "Your app may not use information gained through the use of the HealthKit framework for advertising or similar services." / "You can't sell information gained through HealthKit to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers." / "You must also provide a privacy policy for any app that uses the HealthKit framework."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/protecting-user-privacy.md
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (Apple doc, Copyright 2026)
- confidence: high

### [4] HealthKit 读授权不可探测拒绝状态；用户可只授近期时间窗而非全量历史，App 只能正向识别「受限授权」
- quote: "your app doesn't know whether someone granted or denied permission to read data from HealthKit. If they denied permission, attempts to read data from HealthKit return only samples that your app successfully saved to the HealthKit store." / "people can choose to grant your app access to only a limited window of recent data rather than their full history... Limited authorization is the only authorization state your app can positively identify."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/authorizing-access-to-health-data.md
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (Apple doc, Copyright 2026)
- confidence: high

### [5] App Store 审核红线：医疗类 App 加严审查；不得宣称仅用设备传感器测血压/体温/血糖/血氧等；须提醒用户咨询医生；有监管许可须提交证明链接
- quote: "Medical apps that could provide inaccurate data or information, or that could be used for diagnosing or treating patients may be reviewed with greater scrutiny." / "apps that claim to take x-rays, measure blood pressure, body temperature, blood glucose levels, or blood oxygen levels using only the sensors on the device are not permitted." / "Apps should remind users to check with a doctor in addition to using the app and before making medical decisions.If your medical app has received regulatory clearance, please submit a link to that documentation with your app."
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (living guideline; fetched 2026-09-23)
- confidence: high

### [6] HealthKit 只能用于健康健身目的并与「健康」App 集成；禁止基于健康医疗数据（含 HealthKit API）做定向/行为广告
- quote: "HealthKit should be used for health and fitness purposes and integrate with the Health app." / "may not engage in targeted or behavioral advertising based on sensitive user data such as health/medical data (e.g. from the HealthKit APIs)"
- url: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (living guideline; fetched 2026-09-23)
- confidence: high

### [7] Android 官方健康样本仓库明确：新集成优先 Health Connect（读/写/共享健康健身数据），Health Platform 已被优先替代；Health Connect 样本要求 API 27+ 与 Health Connect APK（com.google.android.apps.healthdata）
- quote: "Health Connect: Samples to help you with reading, writing and sharing health, fitness and wellness data using Health Connect." / "developers should note that Health Connect should be used in preference to Health Platform for new integrations." / "An Android device or emulator running API level 27 or greater" / "The Health Connect APK (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.healthdata)"
- url: https://github.com/android/health-samples
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (AOSP sample repo; fetched 2026-09-23)
- confidence: high

### [8] Health Connect 权限按数据类型拆分 READ_/WRITE_ 命名（如 android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE / WRITE_STEPS），并有 READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND；须处理 androidx.health.ACTION_SHOW_PERMISSIONS_RATIONALE 以展示隐私政策，Android 14 另需 VIEW_PERMISSION_USAGE + HEALTH_PERMISSIONS
- quote: `<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE"/> ... android.permission.health.WRITE_STEPS ... android.permission.health.READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND" />` / "The app needs to handle this intent and display an appropriate privacy policy explaining how the user's data will be used and handling. This intent is sent to the app when the user clicks on the 'privacy policy' link in the Health Connect permissions dialog." / `<action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW_PERMISSION_USAGE"/><category android:name="android.intent.category.HEALTH_PERMISSIONS"/>`
- url: https://github.com/android/health-samples/blob/main/health-connect/HealthConnectSample/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024 (Copyright 2024 The Android Open Source Project)
- confidence: high

### [9] PIPL 自 2021-11-01 生效，是中国第一部国家级个人信息保护专门法；与 CSL（2017-06-01）/ DSL（2021-09-01）构成三大支柱
- quote: "Most significantly, the PIPL came into effect on November 1, 2021. The PIPL is the first comprehensive, national–level personal information protection law in the PRC." / "the three main pillars of the personal information protection framework in the PRC are the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), the Cybersecurity Law (CSL), and the Data Security Law (DSL)."
- url: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CN
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-02-13 (page last modified)
- confidence: high

### [10] PIPL 下「医疗健康信息」明确属于敏感个人信息；处理敏感个人信息须单独同意（如单独勾选框），且须做个人信息保护影响评估（PIPIA）并留存记录至少 3 年
- quote: "The PIPL defines sensitive personal information as information that, once leaked or illegally used, will easily lead to infringement of human dignity or harm to the personal or property safety of a natural person, including (but not limited to): ... medical health information" / "the separate consent of the data subject (e.g. by using separate consent tick boxes) before conducting certain processing activities, namely: processing sensitive personal information" / "The PIPL requires data controllers to undertake personal information protection assessments (PIPIA), and to retain the results and processing records (for at least three years), in the following circumstances: processing of sensitive personal information"
- url: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CN
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-02-13
- confidence: high

### [11] 健康数据出境（个人 AI 管家上架海外的硬约束）：自当年 1 月 1 日起累计向境外提供敏感个人信息达 1 万人须过 CAC 安全评估；不足 1 万人走中国版标准合同（SCC）备案+PIPIA；均需单独同意
- quote: "a data controller intends to transfer sensitive personal information of more than 10,000 data subjects overseas since 1 January of the year when the calculation is conducted" (CAC security assessment) / "a data controller intends to transfer sensitive personal information of fewer than 10,000 data subjects overseas since 1 January of the year when the calculation is conducted" (China SCCs filing) / "notice and separate, explicit consent has been given / obtained from the data subjects"
- url: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CN
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-02-13
- confidence: high

### [12] 2025-11-01 起生效的《数据安全技术 敏感个人信息处理安全要求》要求按处理场景与对个人的影响个案判断是否敏感，不能只对照清单；个人开发者处理手环健康数据宜按敏感个人信息对待
- quote: "under the Data Security Technology – Security Requirements for Processing of Sensitive Personal Information (effective from 1 November, 2025), when assessing whether certain personal information constitutes sensitive personal information, data controllers must now focus more on the processing context, and the impact of the processing activities on data subjects, rather than referring to any prescribed lists of sensitive personal information. As such, going forward a case-by-case analysis may be required to identify sensitive personal information."
- url: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CN
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-02-13
- confidence: high

### [13] PIPL 长臂管辖：在中国境外处理境内自然人个人信息、以向其提供产品/服务或分析评估其行为为目的的，同样适用；个人开发者若服务中国用户即便服务器在海外也落入规制
- quote: "The PIPL has extra–territorial effect, and applies both to: data processing activities within the PRC; and processing of PRC residents' data outside of PRC where: for the purposes of providing products or services to PRC residents; for analytics or evaluation of behavior of PRC residents; or for any other reasons as required by law or regulations."
- url: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CN
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-02-13
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- Bing（含 ensearch=1）对 "Health Connect permissions"、"PIPL 敏感个人信息" 等技术/法律查询几乎只返回词典与门户噪声，不可用作检索入口。
- developer.android.com 全路径 Transport error（含 get-started / data-types / HealthPermission reference），Health Connect 官方指南页未能直接抓取；能力面改由 AOSP health-samples 仓库与 AndroidManifest 权限声明还原。
- gov.cn / npc.gov.cn / cac.gov.cn 的 PIPL 原文与「移动互联网应用程序信息服务管理规定」（App 备案）多条 URL 404 或 DNS 失败；中国 App 备案/ICP 与应用商店（华为/小米/OPPO）健康类审核细则未取得一手原文。
- play.google.com/about/health-fitness 与 support.google.com Google Play 健康类政策页 Transport error，Play 侧健康 App 政策未能引用一手文本。
- 互联网诊疗监管细则 / 医疗器械软件分类界定（NMPA）目录页未能打开；「医疗诊断边界」在境内的监管定性仅能由 Apple 1.4.1 间接侧证，缺 NMPA 一手依据。
- Apple HealthKit「临床记录」要求（NSHealthRequiredReadAuthorizationTypeIdentifiers ≥3 种）已读到，但与消费级手环场景关联弱，未单列成 finding。
